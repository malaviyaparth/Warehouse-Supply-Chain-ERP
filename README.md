# Warehouse-Supply-Chain-ERP

# Project Proposal

## Project Title

**Warehouse and Supply Chain Management System**

---

# Problem Statement

Many small and medium-sized businesses still manage warehouse inventory and supply chain operations using spreadsheets or manual record-keeping. These traditional methods often lead to inaccurate inventory tracking, delayed order processing, stock shortages, duplicate records, and poor communication between warehouse staff and suppliers. As businesses grow, manual management becomes inefficient and increases operational costs.

A centralized web-based Warehouse and Supply Chain Management System is required to automate inventory tracking, supplier management, purchase orders, stock movement, and order management while providing real-time visibility into warehouse operations.

---

# Objectives

* Develop a centralized warehouse management platform.
* Maintain accurate inventory records.
* Automate stock updates and warehouse operations.
* Manage suppliers and purchase orders efficiently.
* Track incoming and outgoing products.
* Improve communication between warehouse staff and administrators.
* Generate inventory and sales reports.
* Reduce manual errors and paperwork.
* Improve operational efficiency through automation.

---

# Existing System

Currently, many organizations manage warehouse operations manually using spreadsheets, paper records, or disconnected software systems.

### Limitations

* Manual inventory updates
* Stock inaccuracies
* Duplicate records
* Slow order processing
* Difficult supplier management
* Limited reporting
* No real-time inventory visibility
* Time-consuming stock audits
* Increased chances of human error

---

# Proposed System

The proposed Warehouse and Supply Chain Management System is a web-based application that digitizes warehouse operations by providing centralized inventory management, supplier management, purchase order tracking, stock movement monitoring, and order processing.

The system enables administrators and warehouse staff to monitor inventory in real time, manage suppliers, process purchase orders, receive and dispatch products, generate reports, and receive notifications for important warehouse activities.

The application aims to improve operational efficiency, reduce manual effort, and provide accurate inventory information for better decision-making.

---
